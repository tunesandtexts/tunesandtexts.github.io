# Sources

Every value in this release keeps its source on its own row (a citation column, a hymnal number or a link). This page lists every input, what was taken from it and on what terms. The full table is SOURCE_AUDIT.csv.

**CC0 scope.** CC0 covers this project's own contributions: the IDs, the links between texts and tunes, the decisions, corrections and notes. Values drawn from the sources below remain subject to those sources' terms.

**Provisional (0.9).** The actions in the audit were settled on 2026-11-19: values drawn from registers and indexes (authors, translators, meters, tune names, composer notes) are kept as bare facts and credited on their own rows. Sources are invited to send corrections or objections.
